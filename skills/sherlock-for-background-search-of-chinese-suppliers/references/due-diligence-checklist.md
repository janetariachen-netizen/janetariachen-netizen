# Due Diligence Checklist

## Purpose

Use this checklist to avoid over-claiming identity and to keep supplier background checks decision-oriented.

## Pass Strategy

1. Strict pass
- Enable only verified-contact mode.
- Do not enable company-domain or B2B expansion.
- Treat this as baseline truth layer.

2. Expanded pass
- Enable company-domain and B2B expansion.
- Treat findings as candidate leads.
- Require explicit evidence URLs.

## Decision Gates

- Gate A: Identity confidence
  - Are strict-pass hits coherent across multiple platforms?
  - Any cross-person collision risk?

- Gate B: Contact quality
  - Is any direct email/phone visible in strict pass?
  - If only expanded contacts exist, label as unverified candidate.

- Gate C: Business intent
  - Do product-intent keywords align with procurement behavior?
  - Any mismatch between profile persona and inferred product interest?

## Output Language

Always separate:
- direct evidence
- expanded candidates
- unresolved risks

Never claim a contact is confirmed owner contact without direct evidence support.
